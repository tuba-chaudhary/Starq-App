# CANCELLATION, REFUND & RESCHEDULING POLICY

## 1. Customer Cancellations
- **Before Partner Assignment:** You may cancel your booking without any penalty if a partner has not yet been assigned to your service. 
- **After Partner Assignment:** Cancellations made after a partner is officially en route may be subject to a nominal cancellation fee to compensate our partners for their fuel and time.
- **During Service:** Once the status is marked as `SERVICE_STARTED`, the booking cannot be cancelled via the app.

## 2. Refund Processing
- We process payments via Razorpay.
- **Refund Eligibility:** If you cancel an eligible booking, the transaction is marked as `REFUND_PENDING` in our systems for manual reconciliation by our operations team to ensure accuracy.
- **Timelines:** Once approved, actual refunds are routed back to your original payment method via Razorpay. Standard bank timelines apply (typically 5-7 business days). We do not guarantee instant refunds.

## 3. Failed Payments
If money is debited from your account but the STARQ app marks the booking as failed, Razorpay's auto-refund mechanism will reverse the transaction within 3-5 business days.

## 4. Rescheduling
If you wish to change your service time, please cancel the existing booking (if eligible for free cancellation) and create a new one.

## 5. Dispute Resolution
If you disagree with a cancellation fee or a denied refund, please contact our Support Team within 24 hours at [Contact Details].
