NOTE: This policy must be reviewed by a qualified Indian legal professional for compliance with the DPDP Act (2023) and IT Rules.


1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email, and phone number when you register.
  • Service Data: Vehicle details, saved addresses, and booking history.
  • Payment Data: We use Razorpay to process payments. STARQ does not directly collect, process, or store raw credit/debit card numbers.
  • Device & App Data: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to send you push notifications regarding your booking status.


    • 2. Location Tracking

  • For Partners: We collect live GPS location data only when you have an active, assigned booking. Tracking strictly stops when the booking is completed or cancelled.
  • For Customers: We do not track your live location. Location permissions are only used to easily pinpoint your vehicle's address during booking.


    • 3. Vehicle Photos

    We may collect photographs of your vehicle before and after the service to document pre-existing damage and verify service quality.

    4. How We Use Your Data

  • To confirm and assign bookings.
  • To process payments and refunds securely.
  • To send transactional notifications.
  • To resolve disputes and provide customer support.


    • 5. Data Sharing and Service Providers

    We do not sell your personal data. We share necessary data only with:
  • Assigned Partners: Name, phone number, and address to fulfill the service.
  • Service Providers: Razorpay (payments), Firebase (notifications), and our cloud hosting provider.


    • 6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

  • You may request account deletion directly through the STARQ app Settings.
  • Retention: Upon deletion, your personal profile (name, phone, email) is anonymized. However, we legally retain financial transaction logs and booking histories to comply with Indian tax and accounting laws.


7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the anonymization of your data. To exercise these rights or file a grievance, please contact our Grievance Officer at [Contact Email].
