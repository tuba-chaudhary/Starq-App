Account Deletion Instructions

Data Retention Notice: While your personal identifiers will be anonymized/erased, financial transaction logs are legally retained for tax and accounting purposes.

How to request account deletion in the app:

  1. Open the STARQ App on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap on Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation dialogue carefully and tap Delete.
  5. Your personal data will be immediately anonymized, your push notification tokens will be revoked, and you will be logged out.

Request deletion via email:

If you no longer have access to the app, you may email our Grievance Officer at [Contact Email] with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Please include the phone number associated with your account.
